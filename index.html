The Conscious Alternative

  • ◎ Shop Second-Hand: Visit thrift stores or use apps like Depop/Poshmark to give clothes a second life.
  • ◎ The 30-Wear Rule: Before buying, ask: "Will I wear this at least 30 times?" If not, don't buy it.
  • ◎ Rent for Events: Use rental services for weddings or parties instead of buying a "one-time" outfit.
  • ◎ Repair & Upcycle: Learn to sew a button or patch a hole to keep garments out of landfills.
