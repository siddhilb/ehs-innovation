Action Checklist

  • Check Registration: Verify your status monthly via vote.gov.
  • Know Your ID: Verify if your state requires a photo ID.
  • Provisional Ballots: You have a right to one if your eligibility is questioned.

The "Stay Safe" Protocol

  • Right to Remain Silent: Clearly state: "I am invoking my 5th amendment right."
  • Search Refusal: State clearly: "I do not consent to a search."
  • Right to Record: You can record in public spaces if you don't interfere.

Protester Rights

  • Public Forums: Sidewalks and parks are generally open for assembly.
  • Dispersal Orders: Police must announce a clear exit path before arrests.

Digital Sovereignty

  • Biometrics: Turn off FaceID/TouchID before high-risk events.
  • Encryption: Use end-to-end encrypted messaging like Signal.
