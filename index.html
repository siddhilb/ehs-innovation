Action Checklist

  • ◎ Check Registration: Many states purge rolls; verify your status monthly.
  • ◎ Know Your ID: Verify if your state requires a photo ID or a secondary proof of address.
  • ◎ Provisional Ballots: If you are turned away, you have a right to a provisional ballot.

The "Stay Safe" Protocol

  • ◎ Right to Remain Silent: You must state clearly: "I am invoking my right to remain silent."
  • ◎ Search Refusal: Unless there is a warrant, say: "I do not consent to a search."
  • ◎ Right to Record: You have a right to record interactions in public spaces.

Protester Rights

  • ◎ Public Forums: Parks and sidewalks are traditionally protected spaces.
  • ◎ Dispersal Orders: Police must provide an exit path before making arrests for "unlawful assembly."
  • ◎ Digital Safety: Use encrypted apps and disable biometrics (FaceID/TouchID) on your phone.

Digital Sovereignty

  • ◎ End-to-End Encryption: Use Signal or WhatsApp for sensitive communications.
  • ◎ Two-Factor Auth: Protect your accounts from state or third-party hacking.
  • ◎ Metadata: Be aware that photos contain location data (EXIF) unless stripped.
